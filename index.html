The skills no job description names

You will work through three skills the hiring reports keep naming: judgment, adaptability, and clarity from ambiguity. For each one, trace it to where you built it, then tag it in one plain sentence.

How each round works

  1. Trace. Pick a prompt and write one memory. It is for your eyes only.
  2. Tag. Turn that memory into one sentence: I [verb] [who or what] so that [result].
  3. Transfer. Choose where your three tags go next.

Your tag does not have to be pretty. It has to run. You can polish it later, like any version 1.0.

These parallels are an analogy, not a formal framework: traceability and debugging, labeling and versioning, transfer learning.

Round 4 · Transfer

Carry it into a new room

Engineers call this transfer learning: take what you learned in one place into a new setting instead of starting over. You have been doing it your whole life.

Walk Into the Room

Picture a room you want to be in a year from now: a lab, a team meeting, an interview, a stage. See the door. Walk in. Who is there? What does that room need? Which of your tags walked in with you?

Who's Waiting for This

Every team has a problem it has not solved. What problem is waiting for someone with your tag? Who would breathe easier if you showed up?

Pack the Bag

Your three tags are packed. Which do you reach for first in the new room, and which one surprised you by being there?

You are not borrowing credibility. You are bringing evidence.
